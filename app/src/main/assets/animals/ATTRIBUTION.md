released under **CC0 1.0 Universal** (public domain dedication; full text in LICENSE-CC0.txt).
Credit is not required; we credit Quaternius anyway.

Files were taken unmodified (GLB) from the CC0 mirror https://github.com/Papyszoo/CC0-Public-Domain-Models
(packs/quaternius-ultimateanimatedanimals, commit 77343cac874f06b73d16ad0063339df7c9ca254c), whose pack.json states licence "CC0".
